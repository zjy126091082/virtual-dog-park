Dog Encyclopedia

Short notes on size, personality and everyday care.

Beagle

Size: Small to medium, about 10 to 11 kg.

Personality: Curious, cheerful and always following its nose.

Care: Two walks a day, a fenced garden, and a weekly ear check.

Golden Retriever

Size: Large, about 25 to 34 kg.

Personality: Gentle, patient and eager to please people.

Care: Long daily exercise, brushing three times a week, and shade on hot days.

Poodle

Size: Toy to standard, about 3 to 25 kg depending on the type.

Personality: Clever, lively and quick to learn new tricks.

Care: Professional grooming every six to eight weeks and daily brushing.
